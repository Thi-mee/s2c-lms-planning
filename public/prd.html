Variable LMS · Product dossier · Reviewed 18 September 2026

The product baseline.

Assigned cohort learning, with explicit authority and preserved learning history.

Derived documentation

This summary is not an implementation specification. Canonical repository sources govern: MVP specification · Domain · Permissions · Features. Identity and the first course-publication path are implemented; learner delivery remains planned. See the current implementation and local setup.

Journey

From invitation to completion.

  1. Operator establishes the organization and a staff-only Administrator.
  2. Authorized Author creates and publishes a course; staff schedule and coordinate a cohort.
  3. Manager or Administrator invites a Learner. Acceptance atomically checks learner capacity.
  4. Coordinator, Administrator or Manager assigns a new learning run.
  5. Learner consumes required lessons and passes every graded quiz in that run.
  6. Completion is recorded and the first course certificate is issued; later completed runs retain their own evidence.

Learning starts from assigned cohorts. There is no MVP self-enrollment catalog, publishing approval queue or sequential lesson lock.

Authority

Privilege is explicitly delegated.

Organization Managers may grant/revoke Learner, Course Author, Cohort Coordinator and Learning Facilitator. They cannot grant/revoke Organization Manager or Administrator or use account edits to gain that authority.

Administrators manage Organization Managers and lower organizational roles. Administrator assignment/removal has a dedicated privileged operation, audit and final-usable-Administrator protection. Resource grants remain distinct from account roles. Consult the canonical matrix for every action.

Evidence

Preserve the run that produced the result.

Lesson progress and submitted assessment evidence are scoped to Enrollment. Resetting an exhausted failed allowance retains old submissions. Re-enrollment starts new progress and allowance. Course summaries do not replace raw history.

Completion requires all required lessons and all graded passes in the current run. Practice quizzes do not count. Certificate identity is an opaque reference; the MVP does not include public anonymous verification.

Communication

Discussion and durable email.

Course/module forums are scoped to a cohort and use sanitized Markdown. Permissioned moderation is audited. Email covers invitations, cohort starts, replies and completion, with persisted retry intent and configured SMTP. A global notification center is deferred.
