# Product goals

> **Status:** Confirmed baseline\
> **Authority:** Canonical — product goals\
> **Updated:** 2026-09-17

## Purpose

Guide implementation priorities for Variable LMS, initially serving s2c.

## Goals

1. Deliver an organization-operated product through immutable OCI releases and versioned deployment packages, with customer-controlled upgrades.
2. Enforce the licensed limit on unique active Learner-entitled accounts without charging extra for staff roles or multiple enrollments.
3. Provide reusable courses, ordered modules, rich-text lessons and external required readings, delivered through assigned cohorts.
4. Evaluate understanding with practice and graded quizzes, preserving enrollment-owned history.
5. Support cohort-scoped course/module discussion and staff oversight.
6. Evaluate run completion and automatically issue the existing user/course-scoped certificate outcome.
7. Make authorization, audit, backup, restore and accessible interaction part of normal delivery quality.

## Non-goals

No course storefront/payments, SCORM/xAPI runtime, native video hosting/transcoding, live classroom implementation, native mobile apps, public self-enrollment, compulsory Kubernetes, or initial microservices. External content/meeting links are allowed. Future capabilities are not implicit MVP requirements.
